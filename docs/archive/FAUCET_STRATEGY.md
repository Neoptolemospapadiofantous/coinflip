## 🎯 Pro Tips to Maximize Success

### 1. IP Address Strategy
- **Use mobile hotspot** if home IP is blocked
- **VPN off** - most faucets block VPN
- **Different browsers** for different faucets
- **Incognito mode** helps reset some limits

### 2. Timing Strategy
- **Early morning** (2am-6am your time) - less traffic
- **Weekdays** vs weekends - try both
- **Wait 24 hours** between same faucet attempts
